§1 — Who We Are · The Promise
A white-glove hosting company for the industries it chooses. The plan includes everything — the site, the SEO, the reviews — and the client never lifts a finger.

The promise: a trades business gets found on Google and never thinks about its website again. One number to call or text; changes handled, confirmation sent back. SLAs: acknowledge under 4 hours · Basic edits live under 2 business days · Business edits under 1 business day · site-down handled immediately. The register: luxury is demonstrated, never self-declared — white-glove onboarding, concierge line, flawless execution; we run and manage the client's entire online presence. The provenance: the canonical stack, not duct tape — WordPress (powers 40%+ of the web; the same software behind WhiteHouse.gov, NASA.gov, and Fortune-500 web properties) on wp.cloud (built by Automattic, WordPress's own maker — the infrastructure behind WordPress.com) with domains on Tucows/OpenSRS (the world's second-largest registrar, the same platform behind Shopify's domains). Future-proof, demonstrated: every Envosta site is built and maintained through an AI-native pipeline today — as AI advances, the dominant platform gets it first, and Envosta manages every step. The roadmap: construction trades now — stickiest, most reachable, highest ticket — then legal, then professional services. An industry qualifies only if it is local-intent, high-ticket, review-driven, and service-area shaped.

§5 — The Gates · Sequence Is Law
Nothing scales until the gate before it is passed.
GATE 0First 10 clients

One trade (HVAC or roofing), one metro cluster, spine running end-to-end. Measure exactly three numbers: true CAC · tickets per client per month · 90-day churn. Nothing about the model changes before client 10.

GATE 1$15K MRR

First VA QA contractor. Every recurring process becomes a written SOP.

GATE 2$50K MRR — the target

Pass requirements: churn ≤ 3%/mo · ≤ 1 ticket/client/mo · CAC paid back ≤ 4 months. First real hire: ops lead.

GATE 3$150K MRR

Self-serve Basic signup goes live on envosta.com. Trade #2 opens with its own 12 Growth spots.

GATE 4$400K MRR

Self-serve must be > 50% of new MRR, or headcount grows. Physics, not preference. The $1M/mo · 5-FTE shape is ≈ 3,000 self-serve Basic + 200 Business + capped Growth across trades: ops/QA lead · content-QA editor · Growth account manager · support lead · founder.

KILL TRIGGERS — IMMEDIATE, NON-NEGOTIABLE

Churn > 5%/mo for 2 consecutive months → freeze all acquisition. Fix retention before selling one more account.

Tickets > 2/client/mo → freeze all promises and features. Fix the product until the number drops.

Any pricing or tier change inside 12 months → does not exist. The ladder is frozen.
